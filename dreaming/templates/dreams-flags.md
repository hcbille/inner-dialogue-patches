# Dream flags

Questions a dream wasn't sure about: places where the notes might no longer match how things are. Each flag is answered during dream review, or asked gently in a session, one at a time (`.therapy/protocols/dream-flags.md`). The next dream applies or drops it, and never decides it without the client.

Format:
```
### YYYY-MM-DD · short title
- **Question:** phrased as a question about the notes
- **Why:** what the notes say, and which sessions
- **Status:** open | asked (YYYY-MM-DD) | answered (YYYY-MM-DD) | dropped (YYYY-MM-DD) | closed (YYYY-MM-DD)
- **Answer:** in the client's words, with the session or review it came from
```

The startup script reads the `## Open` section, so keep the headings as they are.
